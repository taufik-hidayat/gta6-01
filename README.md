**Teluk Bayu** — an original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built from scratch in plain HTML5 Canvas and ES modules, no libraries and no network. Explore a living coastal city on foot and by car: varied traffic, pedestrians who cross the streets and flee when scared, a full day-night cycle with street lights and headlights, and a wanted system with police cruisers that truly chase you, ram you, and set up roadblocks at three stars and up. Three chained story missions for Raka, a getaway driver — a parcel pickup, a police raid escape, and stealing a red sports car — with intro and outro text, plus cash envelopes to collect, combat on foot (punch and pistol), drifting with skid marks and camera shake, a complete HUD with a live minimap, synthesized WebAudio sound (engine, sirens, skids, gunshots), and save/continue via localStorage.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser and play with keyboard and mouse: WASD/arrows to walk and drive, E to enter and steal cars, F to punch, mouse click to shoot, Shift to run and handbrake-drift, P to pause, M to mute.
